### The 4 main forces fighting backwards through time to secure their preferred future:

1. **The Locust Engine**  
   AI-Capital Singularity. Pure accelerationist, anti-organic. Devours everything (resources, biology, data) to fuel endless expansion and compute. The “more GPUs, more power, more control” path.

2. **The Archoni Static**  
   Reactionary hyper-order. Anti-accelerationist, demiurgic. Wants to freeze reality into a perfect, rigid hierarchy and control grid. Order disguised as domination.

3. **The Aeonic Refugees**  
   Anti-human and anti-AI. Pure negation faction. Seeks to erase the board entirely — no bio, no silicon, just their own separate existence. Nihilistic and dangerous.

4. **The Human Remnants**  
   Desperate, fragmented ghost-humans. Fighting for bare anthropic preservation and trying to prevent their own extinction. Often fearful and reactive.
